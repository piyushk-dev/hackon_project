The problem
Customer Obsession

Indian homes run on rhythm.
Smart homes still wait for orders.

A multi-generational household — working parents, two kids, grandparents — issues dozens of commands a day to devices that never learn what happens next.

05:30
Morning pooja
06:15
Geyser for bath
07:30
Pressure-cooker breakfast
08:00
Water motor on
14:00
Power cut
17:00
Tuition hours
18:30
Evening chai

Reactive, not ready

"Alexa, turn on the geyser" — every single morning. The assistant obeys, but the hot water is still 20 minutes late.

Context-blind

It doesn't know a power cut is different during a child's online class, or that grandparents bathe before everyone wakes.

Trust was never earned

No explanations, no way to correct it — so families keep automation switched off and do everything by hand.

Our solution
Invent & SimplifyEarn Trust

Alexa Thinks Ahead — it anticipates the need behind the routine.

Upload last week's activity log. Alexa learns each member's routines and plans today ahead of time — one cognitive loop, running all day.

SENSE

Fuses 10 devices, presence, time of day and household history into one context.

THINK

Reasons about what the home needs next — not what was just said.

EXPLAIN

WHAT TO WATCH FOR
  1. Learn — upload last week's CSV; Bedrock finds each member's routines with confidence scores.
  2. Deploy — a 24-hour day plays out; Alexa acts before each routine: geyser, pre-cooling, security.
  3. Override — one tap says "you were wrong"; the trust score drops and Alexa asks first next time.
  4. Power cut — Alexa triages: the child's online class stays powered, comfort loads shed to inverter.

Bedrock is the brain, not a bolt-on

Claude does the genuinely hard part: anticipation with a rationale. Everything else is plumbing.

Degrades gracefully

An on-device statistical predictor keeps the demo and the home working if the cloud is unreachable.

Explainability is stored, not bolted on
